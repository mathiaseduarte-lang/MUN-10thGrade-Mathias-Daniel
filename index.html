What is a Safe Space?

A safe space is a place where you are not judged and where you are not attacked, either physically or emotionally. It should be somewhere you can feel comfortable and respected.

✓

You are chill

You feel relaxed and comfortable instead of worried or pressured.

☺

You are happy

You can enjoy being there and feel positive about yourself.

★

You can be yourself

You do not have to pretend to be someone else to feel accepted.

About Us

We are a group of people whose main goal is to make people feel better every day. We want to help create an environment where everyone feels respected, comfortable, and supported.
